Three setups. One bot. Here's how it all works.

02 · How it's wired

One program does everything

Data flows in from Webull, the bot decides, and the results flow out to you.

Webull market data1-min footprint bars (Nasdaq volume), price bars, premarket
Your LVLS zonespasted each morning
Footprint bot on the serverChecks every closed 1-minute bar for the three setups, sizes each trade, tracks it in R, places the paper order, and raises an alarm whenever something stops.
Dashboardon your private network
Webull paper accountmorning alerts
Slackcheck, alarms, report

Everything is also saved in the bot's output folder: alerts.csv, outcomes.csv, paper_orders.csv, stops.csv and the daily reports. The evening evaluation reads those.

Why the share minimums are 200.Webull's footprint counts only Nasdaq trades, about a quarter of all volume. The opening and closing auction prints are also removed from the 9:30 and 15:59 bars.
03 · Reading a footprint

Who was in a hurry, at every price

A normal candle shows four prices. A footprint bar also shows how many shares sold into the bid and how many bought at the offer, price by price. All three setups are built from these numbers.

1

Two numbers per price
Left: sellers who hit the bid. Right: buyers who paid the offer.

2

An imbalance
Buying at a price vs selling one tick lower: 700 against 210 is 3.3×, over the 3× needed.

3

A stack
Three prices in a row pass: buyers overran sellers through that whole zone.

4

Too small to count
180 against 60 is 3×, but under 200 shares. Small numbers are noise.

5

Delta
All buying minus all selling. Cumulative delta adds every bar's delta since 9:30.

04 · Setup 1

📈 Stacked imbalance

Trend continuation. Aggressive buyers win at three or more prices in a row inside one bar, at a level, with the session behind them. You go with them. Long shown; shorts are the mirror.

What the bot checks

  • Three or more prices in a row where buying at the offer is at least 3× the selling one tick lower, with at least 200 shares.
  • Longs only above VWAP, shorts only below.
  • The bar touches a level within 3 cents: prior-day POC, VAH or VAL, an LVLS zone, the premarket high or low, or a manual level.
  • The bar's range is no more than 2× the session average.
  • Confirmation: the bar's delta, a close in its strongest 40%, and the session's cumulative delta all agree.

The order

EntryMarket, at the signal bar's close
StopOne tick past the whole bar
TargetsT1 1R · T2 next level, at most 3R
Good for1 bar
Why the close entry.The first version bought at the stack's edge with a 3 to 6 cent stop; slippage ate up to half an R and it won about 7% of the time. The close entry with the stop past the whole bar, plus confirmation, won about 56% on the same bars. On the server, Nov 4 to 10: about +0.07R a trade, +0.16R in the morning. Settings still offers the old edge and retest entries.
05 · Setup 2

🧱 Absorption, with the day's trend

Buyers pile in at the top of a bar but price can't go higher: someone large is selling into them. On a weak day, that's a short. Short shown; longs are the mirror at a low on a strong day.

What the bot checks

  • At least 22% of the bar's buying, and at least 200 shares, traded at the very high.
  • The bar closes at least 2 cents below its high: the buyers got stuck.
  • With the day: below VWAP and session cumulative delta negative (longs: above VWAP and positive).
  • No level is needed in this mode.

The order

EntrySell stop one tick under the bar's low
Stop1.5× the average 1-min range (last 20 bars)
TargetsT1 1R · VWAP or POC beyond it as T2 (≤3R)
Good for3 bars, then cancel
Why trend mode.Fading every absorption at a level won about 21%. Only taking it with the day, with a 1R target and a wider stop, won about 62% in research. On the server Nov 4 to 10: near break-even overall (+0.03R), +0.19R in the morning.
06 · Setup 3

↘️ Delta divergence

A failed push. Price makes a new high, but the bar sold more than it bought and cumulative delta doesn't confirm. Short shown; longs are the mirror at a new low.

What the bot checks

  • The bar makes a new 5-bar high.
  • Its delta is negative, and cumulative delta is below its own 5-bar high.
  • Price is below VWAP, the bar touches a prior-day level, and its range is normal.

The order

EntrySell stop one tick under the bar's low
StopTwo ticks above the bar's high
TargetsT1 1R · yesterday's VAL as T2 (≤3R)
Good for3 bars, then cancel
The weakest setup so far.After 11:30 it lost about 30R over 164 trades (Nov 4 to 10). It holds up better in the morning, one reason paper orders stop at 11:00 from Nov 16.
07 · Managing a trade

Every trade ends one of four ways

Half comes off at T1 (1R) and the stop on the rest moves to your entry. The rest goes to T2, never more than 3R away. The bot scores each alert exactly this way, with 1 cent of slippage each way.

−1R

Stop first. Take it; never move a stop further away.

+0.5R

T1, then back to entry: half at 1R, the rest at breakeven.

+1R

All out at T1: no T2, or the next level is past 3R.

up to +2R

Half at T1, half at T2.

First 3 bars

Delta exit

If cumulative delta swings against you by more than the signal bar's delta, get out at market. Logged as "delta exit".

15:55 · 15:57

End of day

Anything still open is scored flat at 15:55. The paper account is closed out at 15:57.

Size

About 120 shares

0.5% risk of an example $25,000 account, capped so a position never exceeds the account. On $200 stocks with tight stops the real risk is often only $5 to $18.

08 · Risk rules and paper orders

The guardrails

The bot enforces these on itself. When one is hit, it stops taking alerts and raises an alarm you must acknowledge. The loss limits are loose on purpose for the paper test; tighten them before real money.

Daily loss stop10% · $2,500

That plan is done for the day. Was 1.5% until Nov 13.

Losses in a row10

Done for the day. Was 3 until Nov 3.

Open positionsMax 2

One per symbol. Extra alerts are skipped; one alarm a day says so.

Cooldown15 minutes

No new trade in a symbol after it stops out.

Alert hours9:31 – 15:45

Alerts and R tracking all day. Flat at 15:55 (paper 15:57).

Paper orders9:31 – 11:00

From Mon Nov 16. Later alerts still show and are tracked, with no paper order.

No-trade datesSettings

No alerts at all that day. FOMC, CPI, jobs day, earnings.

A halted plan stays halted until tomorrow.Raising the limit in Settings doesn't clear it; only a restart does. Open trades are still managed while halted.

Skip an alert yourself when the stock is halted or gapping on news, the spread is wider than the stop, or you can't place the stop right away. NVDA, AMD, MU and SNDK often move together: holding two is close to doubling one bet.

11 · Alarms and Slack

When the bot stops something

Every stop alarm shows full-screen on the dashboard until you press Acknowledge, and goes to Slack.

🔔criticalPlan stopped for the day

The 10% daily loss or 10 losses in a row was hit. No new alerts or paper orders on that plan today.

Nothing to do; it resets tomorrow.
🔔warningAlerts skipped, positions full

Two positions were open, so an alert was skipped. Raised once a day.

Nothing; the count is on the dashboard.
🔔warningPaper order rejected

Usually CAN_NOT_SELL_SHORT: no shares to borrow, so no short was taken.

Nothing; it's counted in the report.
🔔warningPaper order skipped or a step failed

A cancel or stop move didn't go through. If exits couldn't be placed, the position was closed at market.

Check Webull's paper app shows no stray position.
🔔warningClosed an untracked position

At 15:57 the paper account held something the bot wasn't tracking, and it closed it.

Mention it in the evening.
🔔criticalPaper errors

The paper API is failing repeatedly.

Check the server log if it lasts.
🔔criticalWebull data problem

Requests failing for a minute, or no new bar for 3 minutes. Repeats every 15 minutes; "data back" when it recovers and missed minutes are pulled in.

Usually wait. If it lasts, check the server.

12 · Plans 2 and 3

Two more plans in the same bot

They're tracked separately, so a Plan 1 halt doesn't stop them.

Plan 2 · Profile tab

Session profile setups

Value edge fade: inside yesterday's value, fade the edges back to POC. 80% rule: open outside value, two 30-min closes back inside, target the far edge. Opening range breakout: break of the first hour with delta, defended pullback. Single-print fill: price returns to a thin zone and crosses it.

Plan 3 · Scanner tab

Watchlist delta scanner

Ranks the 12 stocks by delta every poll. Top names above VWAP make the long focus list, bottom names the short list. Big single trades (block prints) become levels for the day. Focus lists don't filter Plan 1 or 2 alerts yet.

13 · Judging results

Pass, drop, or keep collecting

Judge each setup on its own once it has 40 closed trades. Change one setting at a time and give it time. The plan is about 20 trading days of paper with the rules frozen before real money.

Pass
  • Above +0.2R a trade
  • About 45%+ wins
  • Worst drawdown under 6R
Drop
  • Below 0R a trade
  • Under 35% wins
  • Drawdown over 10R
In between
  • Keep paper trading
  • Keep collecting trades

14 · Fixes

When something looks wrong

Dashboard won't load
Usually your private network connection is off, or the bot just restarted. Reconnect, wait a minute, press F5.
Red "not updating" banner
The page lost the bot, or Webull data stopped. Press F5. If it stays, look for a data alarm in Slack.
9:15 says LVLS is still yesterday's list
Today's zones weren't pasted. Paste them in Settings → Level zones now.
9:15 says the paper account is NOT flat
A position or order is left over. Close it in Webull's paper app before 9:30.
No alerts for hours
A plan is halted, it's a no-trade date, or data stopped. Check the plan status line on Live and any alarms.
Changed a setting, nothing happened
The page may be old, or it was a symbol change, which needs a restart. Press F5; restart the bot for symbol changes. The Backtest tab won't change until you press Run backtest or the nightly run.
15 · Terms

Words used in this guide

Footprint barA 1-minute bar showing, at each price, shares sold at the bid and bought at the offer.
Bid / offerSelling at the bid means a seller was in a hurry; buying at the offer means a buyer was.
DeltaBuying at the offer minus selling at the bid, for one bar.
Cumulative deltaEvery bar's delta added up since 9:30.
ImbalanceBuying at a price 3× the selling one tick lower (or the reverse), 200+ shares.
StackThree or more imbalances in a row on one side.
VWAPThe day's volume-weighted average price. Above it the session leans up.
POC · VAH · VALYesterday's busiest price, and the top and bottom of its 70% value area.
LVLS zonesYour daily supply, demand and gap zones per stock. They count as levels and targets.
ROne unit of risk: entry to stop. +1R means you made what you risked.
T1 · T2First target (1R, half off) and second target (next level, at most 3R).
BreakevenStop moved to the entry after T1, so the rest can't lose.
ATRAverage range of the last 20 one-minute bars.
PaperWebull's simulated account: real orders, fake money.
HaltA plan stopped for the day by a risk rule.
