Why the bot keeps losing

The "+$1,718 since Nov 4" was one great day (Nov 5, +$2,202). Without it the main setups lost about $1,200 over eight days. Three things dragged it down: divergence trades, very tight stops, and paper filling worse than the bot's tracking.

Nov 16 to 11:35. Nov 12 was cut short by the old 1.5% daily stop.

Right side vs flipped side
